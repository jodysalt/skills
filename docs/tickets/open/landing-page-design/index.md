# Goal
The page `add-landing-page` writes has a high-end, editorial feel on the first run, so a market test goes live from one interview instead of three rounds of restyling. `add-landing-page` becomes a scaffold: it copies the boilerplate – the page with the house design language as literal CSS and `TODO:` placeholders where the copy goes, the notes, the mocks' stylesheet and index, the workflow – from template files beside the skill, then hands off to `refine-landing-page`, which owns the one interview, the findings, the mock step, the edits and the result. Pages share one design language and differ by the accent and mood the interview sets, so they share quality without sharing identity, and nothing is written in two skills.

# Context
- Evidence: `../breezybooking` needed three follow-up commits after the first run – `5fd00f6` (sticky nav with wordmark and a small call to action, a 3-step strip, a price card with a checklist, section `<h2>`s with a lede, the headline led with the loss the hypothesis tests, an eyebrow naming the audience, an FAQ naming the real card fee), `cc03fcc` (three-column equal-height benefit cards at ≥54rem, screenshots in a fixed 17rem frame, every card carrying a screen) and `d22d89c` (tokens `--paper`, `--ink`, `--sea`, `--gold`; serif display type from the system stack; eyebrow with a hairline; a staged hero in a gradient panel with a 9px ink phone bezel; numbered cards; a dark call-to-action band with a radial glow; hairline FAQ; italic footer line; `clamp()` type scale; `text-wrap: balance`). Its final `docs/landing-page/index.html` is the reference this ticket turns into the house language.
- Cause: `skills/add-landing-page/SKILL.md` step 3 prescribes the plain look – one column, `max-width` around `40rem`, the button a block, "nothing else beyond readable spacing and type sizes". The first run did exactly that.
- Constraints that stand: the *A website builder* non-goal in `docs/vision.md` – one static file, no framework or build step – so no `<link>` and no web font; the display serif comes from the system stack (`"New York", ui-serif, "Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Palatino, Georgia, serif`). The seven section ids and their order stay, since `refine-landing-page` step 3 reads them.
- `refine-landing-page` step 4 keeps the page's shape as `add-landing-page` fixed it and step 5 is `add-landing-page` step 5 copied byte for byte, so a change to either step lands in both skills.
- Glossary: *landing page*, *market test* and *mock* in their defined senses.

# Scope
In:
- **Template files beside the skill carry the boilerplate**: `skills/add-landing-page/templates/index.html`, `skills/add-landing-page/templates/mock.css` and `skills/add-landing-page/templates/mocks-index.html`, copied byte for byte with no substitution. The page template is `../breezybooking`'s final page with its copy replaced by `TODO:` placeholders and its tokens at the warm-paper defaults; the design language is its literal CSS, which the model never re-derives. A change to the look is an edit to a file that opens in a browser. This is the plugin's first skill with a supporting file; `add-skill`'s one-file rule is a drafting rule for new skills and stays as it is.
- **The page template is a full skeleton**: the nav with a generic inline-SVG mark and a `TODO: product name` wordmark linking `#headline` and a small outlined call to action linking `#cta`; the intro with the eyebrow, `h1` and subhead as `TODO:` sentences and `<figure id="hero">` holding an HTML comment and no `<img>`; `#benefits` with its `h2`, lede, three numbered cards each holding `TODO:` copy and no `<img>`, and the 3-step strip with three `TODO:` steps; `#price` with the amount, unit, sentence and a four-item checklist as `TODO:`s; `#cta` with `href=""`, a `TODO:` `h2` and the terms line; `#faq` with three `TODO:` question-and-answer pairs; `#terms` with the three terms lines as `TODO:`s and the brand line; an analytics `TODO:` comment as the last thing before `</head>`; `:root` tokens at the warm-paper defaults under a `/* TODO: look */` marker, so an unset look is a finding. The seven ids and their order are the closed `landing-page` ticket's; the nav and intro wrapper carry no id. `refine-landing-page` adds or removes cards, steps, checklist items and FAQ pairs to match the answers, and removes each `TODO:` as its answer lands.
- `templates/mock.css` is Breezy's `mock.css` restyled to the page's tokens and sans stack in a product-UI idiom – top bar, card, button, table, pill, notice, `.frame.phone` at 390px – under the same `/* TODO: look */` marker; `templates/mocks-index.html` is the mocks index with an empty `<ul>`.
- **`add-landing-page` becomes a scaffold**: step 1 root and guard as now (stops on an existing page, naming `refine-landing-page`); step 2 copies the three templates to `docs/landing-page/index.html`, `docs/landing-page/mocks/mock.css` and `docs/landing-page/mocks/index.html` and writes `docs/landing-page/README.md` from its template with every section a `TODO:` prompt; step 3 writes the workflow when missing, as now; step 4 invokes `jodysalt:refine-landing-page` with an empty argument and the framing "the idea is: {idea}; this is a fresh boilerplate"; step 5 reports what was copied and hands the rest to `refine-landing-page`'s own report. Its step 2 interview and step 5 mock step are removed.
- **`refine-landing-page` owns the one interview, gated by findings.** Its findings already include every `TODO:` in the page, the notes or a mock; the `/* TODO: look */` marker and `TODO:` placeholders in `README.md` join them. Step 4's interview holds the five branches in order – Offer (product's name for the wordmark, who it is for, headline, subhead, benefits, price, call to action with kind, link and terms, FAQ objections), Look (accent, mood), Screens (one per benefit, the first the hero), Test (hypothesis, metric, threshold, window), Analytics (optional snippet) – each with its recommended answer and the call-to-action warning, and a branch is asked only when a finding or the scope raises it: on a fresh boilerplate every branch fires, on a later run only what is raised, and a run stopped mid-interview resumes with the same command because its remaining `TODO:`s still fire. Answers land as edits to the boilerplate's markup, `README.md`'s sections, the `:root` values of `index.html` and `mock.css`, and the mock step for each screen; each `TODO:` goes as its answer lands.
- **Look answers land in CSS.** The Look branch edits the `:root` block of both `docs/landing-page/index.html` and `docs/landing-page/mocks/mock.css`: the mood picks one of three literal value sets written in `refine-landing-page`'s step – *warm paper* (Breezy's values, `#f7f4ed` paper; recommended for consumer, outdoors and craft products), *cool white* (software, B2B) and *dark ink* (developer tools, finance), six surface, ink and line values each – and the accent is one hex the user gives, recommended from the audience, its deep and mist variants derived as a darker shade and a light tint; the `/* TODO: look */` marker goes. `look` joins `refine-landing-page`'s scope words so a later run can re-ask accent and mood. The product's name for the wordmark is an Offer question, recommended from the idea or the repo name; the old interview never asked it and Breezy's wordmark was invented.
- **Tokens**, as the template's `:root` names them: `--surface`, `--surface-deep`, `--panel`, `--ink`, `--ink-soft`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--line-strong`, `--accent`, `--accent-deep`, `--accent-mist`, `--gold` (a fixed warm metallic for the eyebrow rule and the numerals) and `--serif` (the system serif stack `"New York", ui-serif, "Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Palatino, Georgia, serif`).
- **Components the template holds**, lifted from Breezy's `d22d89c`, so the ticket records what the file fixes; the seven section ids and their order stand, and the two wrappers sit outside them – `<nav class="top">` before, `<div class="intro">` around `#headline` and `#hero` – so `refine-landing-page`'s findings still match: a sticky translucent nav with an inline-SVG mark, the wordmark and a small outlined call to action linking `#cta`; an intro grid `1.1fr 0.9fr` at ≥54rem under a radial accent glow; an eyebrow (uppercase, letter-spaced, a hairline before it) naming the audience; `h1` at `clamp(2.7rem, 6.2vw, 4.9rem)` in the serif with `text-wrap: balance`; a staged hero in a gradient panel, a phone screen at 19rem in a 9px ink bezel with a 38px radius and a deep shadow, a desktop screen in a 1px hairline frame with a 14px radius; benefits as an `h2` with a lede over three equal-height cards at ≥54rem, each with an italic gold numeral and its screenshot in a 17rem frame (`object-fit: cover; object-position: left top`); a 3-step strip inside `#benefits`; the price as a deep-surface band holding a card with a 4.6rem serif amount, a small uppercase unit and a hairline checklist with accent ticks; the call to action as an ink band under a radial accent glow with a paper pill button at 20rem and the terms line; the FAQ as a 46rem hairline list; the footer hairline-topped, small and muted, its last line serif italic. Scale: body 17px at 1.65, `h2` at `clamp(2rem, 4.2vw, 3rem)`, `.wrap` 74rem with 1.25rem gutters, sections 5.5rem.
- **Copy rules**, carried in the Offer questions' recommended answers in `refine-landing-page`: the headline leads with the loss or longing the hypothesis tests, never the category; the subhead states the mechanism in one sentence; `h2`s are promises in the audience's words; every benefit is an outcome with a concrete scene and gets a screen, the Screens branch recommending one per benefit as "every benefit"; the three steps are the user's first hour; FAQ answers name real costs and dates; the call to action's `h2` is an invitation and the button's text its kind.
- **`refine-landing-page`'s shape constraints** name the house design language – the template's tokens, components and breakpoint – so an edit keeps it and an added card, step, checklist item or FAQ pair copies the template's markup; the mock step stays there, once, with no copy anywhere; the analytics snippet replaces the head's `TODO:` comment verbatim.
- **Descriptions and rows.** `add-landing-page`: scaffolds the market test from the plugin's templates – the landing page at `docs/landing-page/index.html` in the house design language with placeholders, its notes in `docs/landing-page/README.md`, the mocks' stylesheet and index under `docs/landing-page/mocks/`, the workflow when missing – then hands off to `refine-landing-page`, which interviews and fills it. `refine-landing-page`: gains "reports every placeholder" before "what the notes imply the page lacks". Both README rows are their first sentences.
- `docs/glossary.md` gains *design language* and *boilerplate*. `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` to `0.23.0`.

Out:

## Strategic fit
Delivers *Market test before building* by making the page the test ships worth sending traffic to on the first run, so the test starts the day the interview ends instead of three restyles later; stands on *Decisions evaporate*, since the design decisions of the Breezy rounds are written into the skill rather than left in that repo's history, and on *Trim with the 80/20 principle*, carrying the look as one file copied rather than a spec re-derived, and on *One thing, then stop*, since `add-landing-page` scaffolds and `refine-landing-page` interviews with nothing written twice. It respects *A website builder*: one static file, no font, no framework, no build step; the templates are a design asset copied in, not a toolchain.
