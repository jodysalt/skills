# Goal
A user plans with one kind of thing under the vision. A bet in `docs/vision.md` is sliced into `feat` tickets, and any ticket can be broken into sub-tickets named `{slug}--{sub}`, of any type, to any depth. One folder, one lifecycle, one template family and one set of skills cover everything from a one-line fix to a four-month push. The initiative layer and its three skills go, no skill appends a path to a parent or rewrites one on close, and `deliver-brief` turns every brief into one ticket and grows it by sub-tickets until its acceptance criteria are met.

# Context
- Today the chain is bet, initiative, ticket, task. A `feat` tags an initiative with `initiative:`; `add-ticket` appends its path to the initiative's `## Tickets` list; `close-ticket` rewrites that path on close and `close-initiative` moves the initiative and deletes its bet from `vision.md` when no open initiative cites it. Thirteen skills mention initiatives or bets: `add-initiative`, `refine-initiative` and `close-initiative` are about them; `add-ticket`, `refine-ticket`, `add-tasks`, `complete-task`, `close-ticket`, `wrap-up-worktree`, `setup-skills`, `refine-vision`, `start-planning-session` and `deliver-brief` read or write them.
- The evidence for one layer fewer is the first user's repo: 13 initiatives under 5 bets over six months, 63 closed tickets tagged to an initiative, and one bet with seven initiatives in sequence, two of them abandoned. Its bet text was rewritten as pushes shipped while the initiative under it held the ticket list, so the two were edited as one thing. A bet is the stable direction; a ticket, with its sub-tickets, is the push that closes.
- `close-initiative`'s rule deletes a bet as soon as no open initiative cites it. That repo had a four-day gap in September with no open initiative under its main bet. After this ticket a bet is only ever retired by hand through `refine-vision`.
- Both repos have no open ticket today; this one has no open initiative and the first user's has three. Closed tickets and initiatives are project memory and stay where they are.
- Flat beats nested. A sub-ticket at `docs/tickets/open/{slug}--{sub}/` moves to `closed/` on its own; a folder inside its parent's would move with the parent. Sorting the open folder groups sub-tickets under their parent, since the bare slug sorts before its prefixed children. Git branch and worktree names take `--`, and `wrap-up-worktree` already finds a ticket branch by matching the branch name to `docs/tickets/open/<branch>/`.
- A parent and its sub-tickets split one change between them. The parent's own `tasks.md` holds whatever no sub-ticket covers, which may be nothing, so a parent is landed when its own tasks are done and closed when its sub-tickets are too. Those are two moments, and today `wrap-up-worktree` treats them as one.
- `add-ticket` step 2 sends a multi-ticket push to `add-initiative`, step 3 is an initiative gate, step 6's templates carry `initiative:`, step 8 appends to the list. `refine-ticket` steps 2, 4 and 6 read, check and fix the same things. `add-tasks` step 1 and `complete-task` step 2 read the initiative for context; `add-tasks` creates `tasks.md` before drafting any entry. `close-ticket` step 3 greps `docs/initiatives docs/tickets` and step 4 mentions `close-initiative`; `wrap-up-worktree` step 8 does too. `setup-skills` creates `docs/initiatives/{open,closed}/`, and its vision template and `CLAUDE.md` block describe initiatives; `refine-vision` reads `docs/initiatives/open/*.md` for bet citations. `start-planning-session`'s description names `add-initiative` and `refine-initiative` as callers.
- `deliver-brief` step 3's scout sizes a brief as one shippable change or "a bet and an initiative" and the root ratifies; step 4 runs an `add-initiative` stage for the second case; step 5 ends a lone ticket's run after `wrap-up-worktree` and sends a ticket under an initiative to the judge; step 6's judge reads the initiative's `## Success metrics` and `## Tickets` list; the done path runs a `close-initiative` stage; a resume requires exactly one file in `docs/initiatives/open/`. `wrap-up-worktree` closes the ticket before the judge runs, which is fine while the judge decides about an initiative and wrong once it decides about the ticket itself.
- The *Evals for the risky skills* bet in `vision.md` names `close-initiative`. After this ticket that skill is gone; the bet's text is a `vision.md` edit for `refine-vision`, surfaced and not made here.
- `plugin.json` is `0.7.0` and bumps on any change to a skill's behaviour. `README.md` lists every skill in a table and has a Workflow paragraph; this repo's `CLAUDE.md` holds the block `setup-skills` writes. `claude plugin validate . --strict` and `claude plugin validate skills --strict` gate the manifest and frontmatter.

# Scope

In:
- **The `bet:` tag.** `feat` and `spike` tickets cite a bet by its exact `###` title under `## Strategic bets`. A `feat` with no bet still stands on its own on the user's say-so. Other types carry no tag. `initiative:` is written nowhere and read nowhere.
- **Sub-tickets.** Any ticket may have sub-tickets, each a ticket of any type at `docs/tickets/open/{slug}--{sub}/`, where `{slug}` is an open ticket. A sub-ticket inherits its parent's bet and carries no `bet:`; a `feat` sub-ticket's `## Strategic fit` names its parent in one line and the parent traces to the vision. `add-ticket` refuses a `--` prefix that names no open ticket. A ticket's sub-tickets are `docs/tickets/*/{slug}--*`; there is no list to maintain and no depth limit.
- **Sizing.** `add-ticket` stops offering `add-initiative`. A change too large for one backlog is still one ticket; its author breaks sub-tickets off as the breakdown shows the need.
- **Context for the loop.** `add-tasks` and `complete-task` read a sub-ticket's parents up the chain where they read an initiative today. `add-tasks` on a ticket with sub-tickets reads their `index.md` too, writes entries only for what no sub-ticket covers, and creates `tasks.md` only when it has something to append; when the sub-tickets cover everything it says so and writes nothing.
- **Closing.** `close-ticket` refuses a ticket while `docs/tickets/open/{slug}--*` is non-empty, listing the open sub-tickets. Otherwise it moves the folder, keeps rewriting live paths in other ticket bodies and task entries, and no longer greps `docs/initiatives`. On a sub-ticket it says whether the parent has any open sub-ticket left and never closes the parent.
- **Landing.** `wrap-up-worktree` on a ticket branch whose tasks are all done but which has open sub-tickets squashes, merges and removes the worktree without closing the ticket, and says why. With no open sub-ticket it closes as today.
- **`refine-ticket`.** Reads a sub-ticket's parents and the vision's bets; flags a `bet:` no heading matches, a tag on a sub-ticket, a `--` prefix with no open parent, a `feat` sub-ticket whose fit does not name its parent; a `bet:` retag is a user decision as `initiative:` was.
- **Vision and scaffold.** `setup-skills` stops creating `docs/initiatives/`; its vision template's *Strategic bets* prompt and its `CLAUDE.md` block describe bets sliced into tickets and sub-tickets. `refine-vision` reads open tickets' `bet:` tags in place of open initiatives, checks them against the `###` headings, rewrites the tags when a bet is renamed, and retires a bet only when the user says it is spent.
- **`deliver-brief`.** Every brief becomes one ticket. The scout reports its type and, for a `feat`, the bet that covers it or that none does; a brief with no bet still gets one through `refine-vision`. The stages are the scout, `refine-vision`, `add-ticket`, `add-tasks`, `complete-tasks`, the judge and `close-ticket`. The judge runs on a ticket when its own backlog is done and before it lands, in its worktree, reading its `# Acceptance criteria`, its sub-tickets and each spike's `findings.md`: unmet criteria pick a sub-ticket, drafted by an `add-ticket` stage on that ticket's own branch and committed there, so `wrap-up-worktree` then lands the branch without closing; met criteria let `wrap-up-worktree` close it. When a sub-ticket closes, the judge runs on its parent from the main checkout, and a pick there is drafted in a planning session. The run ends when the brief's ticket closes or the ticket cap stops it. A resume requires exactly one open ticket with no `--` in its slug and takes its cursor from the deepest open ticket under it.
- **Removal.** Delete `skills/add-initiative/`, `skills/refine-initiative/` and `skills/close-initiative/`; drop their `README.md` rows; rewrite the README Workflow paragraph, the rows of every skill whose description changes, this repo's `CLAUDE.md` block and `start-planning-session`'s description; remove `docs/initiatives/open/` here and keep `docs/initiatives/closed/` as history. No skill's text mentions an initiative afterwards.
- `plugin.json` to `0.8.0`.

Out:
- Migrating the first user's repo. Its three open initiatives become `feat` tickets with `bet:` by hand; its closed initiatives and tagged tickets stay as they are.
- Rewriting this repo's closed initiatives or closed tickets. They are project memory.
- Editing `vision.md`: the *Evals for the risky skills* bet's mention of `close-initiative` is surfaced for the user.
- A parent type, a `status` field, a list of sub-tickets in any file, a nested layout, or a transitional period that reads `initiative:`.
- Eval suites.
- Pushing.

# Acceptance criteria
- In a throwaway repo, `/jodysalt:setup-skills` creates no `docs/initiatives/`, and the vision template and the `CLAUDE.md` block mention bets, tickets and sub-tickets and never an initiative.
- `/jodysalt:add-ticket` drafts a `feat` tagged `bet:` with an exact bet title, pushes back once when none fits and drafts a standalone `feat` on the user's say-so; for a change spanning several tickets it drafts one ticket and never offers `add-initiative`; drafts a `feat`, a `fix` and a `spike` named `{slug}--{sub}` under an open ticket with no `bet:` and, for the `feat`, a `## Strategic fit` naming the parent; drafts `{slug}--{sub}--{leaf}`; stops and lists the open tickets for `{typo}--{sub}` or a prefix naming a closed ticket.
- `/jodysalt:add-tasks` on a sub-ticket reports the parents it read. On a parent whose sub-tickets cover everything it creates no `tasks.md` and says so; on a parent they cover in part it writes entries only for the rest, then the gate.
- `/jodysalt:close-ticket` on a ticket with an open sub-ticket stops and lists it; with every sub-ticket closed it moves the folder and reports the paths it rewrote in ticket bodies and task entries; on a parent's last open sub-ticket it says the parent has none left and leaves it open.
- `/jodysalt:wrap-up-worktree` from a ticket worktree with every task done and an open sub-ticket leaves the base one commit ahead, the worktree and branch gone, the ticket under `open/`, and says why; with no open sub-ticket it closes as today; with a pending task it stops and changes nothing.
- `/jodysalt:refine-ticket` flags `bet: Nonsuch`, a `bet:` on a sub-ticket, and a sub-ticket whose parent folder was moved to `closed/` by hand.
- `/jodysalt:refine-vision` reports a `bet:` on an open ticket that no `###` heading matches, rewrites the tags when the user renames a bet, and never removes a bet the user did not retire.
- In a throwaway repo with the plugin installed, `/jodysalt:deliver-brief <brief>` for a small app ends with no human turn with one closed ticket for the brief, every sub-ticket closed under `docs/tickets/closed/{slug}--*/`, no `docs/initiatives/`, a passing full suite and one squashed commit per planning session and per ticket branch on `main`. A one-change brief ends with one closed ticket and no sub-ticket. Interrupted mid-sub-ticket and run again with no brief, it resumes in that sub-ticket's worktree at its next pending task.
- `grep -rn initiative skills README.md CLAUDE.md` finds nothing; `skills/add-initiative/`, `skills/refine-initiative/` and `skills/close-initiative/` are gone; `docs/initiatives/open/` is gone and `docs/initiatives/closed/` is unchanged.
- `README.md` rows match every changed description, `plugin.json` is `0.8.0`, and both `claude plugin validate` commands pass with `--strict`.
- Nothing is pushed.

# Implementation notes
- `add-ticket`: the `## Ticket` section names the `{slug}--{sub}` form; step 2 drops the initiative offer and says a change too large for one backlog is one ticket broken into sub-tickets as needed; step 3 becomes a bet gate for `feat` and `spike`, reading `## Strategic bets` and matching by exact title, skipped for a sub-ticket; step 5 checks `docs/tickets/open/{slug}/` exists for a `--` prefix; step 6 writes `bet:` in place of `initiative:` in both templates, neither for a sub-ticket, and a one-line `## Strategic fit` prompt naming the parent for a `feat` sub-ticket; step 7's framing says a sub-ticket's fit traces to its parent; step 8 goes. The rules say the fit traces to `vision.md` or, for a sub-ticket, to its parent, and drop "Initiatives are virtual".
- `refine-ticket`: step 2 reads the parent chain and `## Strategic bets`; step 4's frontmatter bullet checks `bet:` against the headings, a tag on a sub-ticket and a `--` prefix against `docs/tickets/open/`, and the `## Tickets` list finding goes; step 6 and the rules say `bet:` where they said `initiative:`.
- `add-tasks` step 1 reads each parent's `index.md` up the chain and every `docs/tickets/*/{slug}--*/index.md`, and creates `tasks.md` only after step 5 leaves at least one entry; a `Coverage` bullet under step 2 says an entry a sub-ticket covers is not written. `complete-task` step 2 reads the parent chain where it read the initiative.
- `close-ticket`: step 1 gains a guard on `ls -d docs/tickets/open/{slug}--*`; step 3's grep scopes to `docs/tickets`; step 4's `close-initiative` sentence becomes the parent sentence; the rule about `initiative:` being a slug goes.
- `wrap-up-worktree`: step 2's ticket guard splits into the tasks check, a stop, and the sub-ticket check, a note; step 3 closes only with no open sub-ticket; step 8 says when the ticket was left open and why; "never closes an initiative" becomes "never closes a parent with open sub-tickets".
- `setup-skills`: step 1 loses the two initiative directories; the vision template's *Strategic bets* prompt says a bet is sliced into `feat` tickets that cite it by exact title with `bet:`, broken into sub-tickets as needed; the `CLAUDE.md` block names `add-ticket` alone for drafting. `refine-vision`: step 2 reads `bet:` from `docs/tickets/open/*/index.md` frontmatter; the dangling-citation finding, the rename rule and the shape rule follow; "`close-initiative` retires a spent bet" becomes "a bet is retired here when the user says it is spent".
- `deliver-brief`: step 3's scout drops the sizing and reports type and bet; the resume rule finds the one open ticket without `--` and walks `docs/tickets/open/{slug}--*` to the deepest open ticket; step 4 drops the `add-initiative` stage; step 5 runs the judge after `complete-tasks` and before `wrap-up-worktree`, in the ticket's worktree, a pick there being an `add-ticket` stage on the ticket branch with the root declining the planning-session offer, then `jodysalt:commit`, then `wrap-up-worktree`, then step 4 for the new sub-ticket; done lets `wrap-up-worktree` close; after a sub-ticket closes the judge runs on the parent from the main checkout and a pick is drafted in a planning session as today, with done a planning session that runs a `close-ticket` stage on the parent and commits. The stage list, `## Caps` and the report follow.
- `start-planning-session`'s description drops `add-initiative` and `refine-initiative` from its caller list. Descriptions are frontmatter, so `--strict` checks them; keep each skill's trigger phrases and "never" clauses.

# Rollout
- Bump `plugin.json` to `0.8.0`; merging to `main` is the release and users on auto-update get it at their next session. It is a breaking release for a repo that has initiatives, and the `README.md` says how to migrate: turn each open initiative into a `feat` ticket with `bet:`, rename its open tickets `{slug}--{sub}`, leave `closed/` alone, and delete `docs/initiatives/open/`. Fallback is reverting the commit.
- The first user's repo migrates by hand afterwards: three open initiatives and no open tickets.
- This ticket was drafted by hand with a `bet:` tag, since today's `add-ticket` writes `initiative:`. It is implemented as one ticket on one branch, with no sub-tickets of its own.

## Strategic fit
Advances *Dogfooding*: the method's largest change to itself arrives as a ticket the loop implements. It serves *Conventions are hard to adopt* from `vision.md`, since a newcomer learns one folder and a naming rule instead of four levels and three extra skills, and it moves the *Adoption beyond the first user* bet closer for the same reason. It follows *One opinionated method* and *Trim with the 80/20 principle* by deleting three skills and a list-rewriting step rather than adding a type or a mode. It respects the *Several plugins* non-goal, since the chain stays one plugin, and shrinks what the *Evals for the risky skills* bet has to cover.
