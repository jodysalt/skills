# Goal
`refine-landing-page` sets the page's look: a **Look** branch in its interview, between Offer and Screens, asks the accent – one hex, recommended from the audience – and the mood – one of *warm paper*, *cool white* and *dark ink* – and lands the answer as the `:root` values of `docs/landing-page/index.html` and `docs/landing-page/mocks/mock.css`, removing the `/* TODO: look */` marker from both. The three moods are literal value sets written in the step, so the model copies values and never re-derives them, and the accent's deep and mist variants are `color-mix()` expressions of the one hex, so the edit is one value. `look` joins the scope words so a later run re-asks the look on a page whose look is set. After this slice a fresh boilerplate run ends with no `TODO:` anywhere, the page and the mocks sharing the chosen mood and accent, and step 7 no longer says the marker is waiting.

  - **The accent** – one hex, recommended from the audience the eyebrow names: a sea green such as `#0b7a75` for coastal, outdoor and travel products, a deep blue such as `#1d4ed8` for finance and B2B software, a warm orange such as `#c2410c` for food and hospitality, a plum such as `#6d28d9` for creative tools; on *dark ink* a brighter hue of the same family, such as `#3b82f6` for a blue, since the accent sits on dark surfaces. The question quotes the current `--accent`.

The question quotes the current `--surface`.
- **The three mood value sets**, written in the Look branch as literal lines the model copies, each giving the eight surface, ink and line tokens; `--gold` and `--serif` are fixed and never asked or changed:
  - *warm paper*: `--surface: #f7f4ed; --surface-deep: #efe9dd; --panel: #fffdf8; --ink: #0e1b21; --ink-soft: #33444c; --muted: #6a7980; --line: rgba(14, 27, 33, 0.12); --line-strong: rgba(14, 27, 33, 0.22);`

  - *dark ink*: `--surface: #0f1419; --surface-deep: #151b22; --panel: #1a222b; --ink: #eef1f4; --ink-soft: #c5ced6; --muted: #8b98a5; --line: rgba(238, 241, 244, 0.12); --line-strong: rgba(238, 241, 244, 0.22);`

- **The accent derivation**, three lines written in the step: `--accent: {hex};`, `--accent-deep: color-mix(in srgb, var(--accent) 70%, black);` for *warm paper* and *cool white* and `color-mix(in srgb, var(--accent) 70%, white);` for *dark ink*, and `--accent-mist: color-mix(in srgb, var(--accent) 18%, var(--surface));` for every mood. The edit is one value – the hex – and the mood decides the mixing partner; computed hexes are not written. On *warm paper* the two expressions replace the template's `#064f4b` and `#dcebe7` as well, so the three `:root` shapes are the same.
