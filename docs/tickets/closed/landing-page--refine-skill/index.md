# Goal
`/jodysalt:refine-landing-page [section names, a mock slug, or 'result']` exists, in the shape of the other `refine-*` skills: it reads the landing page at `docs/landing-page/index.html`, the market test's notes in `docs/landing-page/README.md` and the mocks under `docs/landing-page/mocks/`, reports what the notes imply and the page lacks, interviews through `grill-me` and edits the page and the notes in place as answers land, writing or rewriting a mock with its screenshot when an answer needs a screen, and, with `result` or when the user says the test is over, fills `## Result` and `## Decision` in `README.md`. A user whose page is live can improve it against its hypothesis without touching the markup by hand, and the test ends with its numbers written beside the page, where `refine-vision` reads them.

# Scope
In:
- **`skills/refine-landing-page/SKILL.md`**, new, from `add-skill`'s template.
- **Frontmatter.** `name: refine-landing-page`; a `description` in three parts – it revisits the landing page at `docs/landing-page/index.html` and improves it in place against the market test's notes in `docs/landing-page/README.md`: reads the page, the notes and the mocks under `docs/landing-page/mocks/`, reports what the notes imply and the page lacks, interviews the user one question at a time, edits the page as answers land, writes or rewrites a mock with its screenshot when an answer needs a screen, and records the result and the decision in `README.md` when the test is over; the trigger phrases "refine the landing page", "record the test result", "the landing page needs X" and `/refine-landing-page`; never creates the page, never edits the workflow, never pushes, never commits – and `argument-hint: "[section names, a mock slug, or 'result' – defaults to the whole page]"`.
- **`## Scope`** on `$ARGUMENTS`: one or more section names, matched case-insensitively against the page's section ids (`headline`, `hero`, `benefits`, `price`, `cta`, `faq`, `terms`) and `README.md`'s `##` headings (`hypothesis`, `metric`, `threshold`, `window`, `call to action`); a mock slug, with or without its `mock-` prefix, matching `mocks/mock-{slug}.html`; or `result`, which runs the findings and then step 6. With names, the findings and the interview cover only those sections or that mock. Empty, the whole page. A name that matches nothing: list the names and ask.
- **Steps:**
  1. *Root and guard.* The root is `git rev-parse --show-toplevel`, else the working directory. If `docs/landing-page/index.html` is missing, stop and point at `jodysalt:add-landing-page`; this skill improves a page, it never creates one. Read `docs/glossary.md` when it exists, a missing or template-only file counting as an empty glossary, and use each defined term in its defined sense in the edits and the report, never redefining one in passing.
  2. *Explore before asking.* Read `docs/landing-page/index.html`, `docs/landing-page/README.md` when present, `docs/landing-page/mocks/index.html`, `docs/landing-page/mocks/mock.css` and every `docs/landing-page/mocks/mock-*.html` when present, and `ls docs/landing-page/mock-*.png` for the screenshots on disk; take today's date from `date +%F`. Recommended answers come from these.
  3. *Report findings before editing anything*, each named by section, file or slug:
     - Each of the seven section ids `add-landing-page` writes that the page lacks (`#headline`, `#hero`, `#benefits`, `#price`, `#cta`, `#faq`, `#terms`), and `README.md` missing, or each of its five `##` headings – `Hypothesis`, `Metric`, `Threshold`, `Window`, `Call to action` – missing or empty. The two the page mirrors are checked against it: `## Call to action` names a link that differs from `#cta`'s `href`, and `## Hypothesis` claims a benefit no `<h3>` in `#benefits` names.
     - `#cta`'s `<a>` with `href=""` or no `href`.
     - `#hero` with no `<img>`, and each benefit – an `<h3>` in `#benefits` – with no `<img>` before the next `<h3>` or the section's end.
     - Each `<img src>` on the page or in `mocks/index.html` with no file on disk at `docs/landing-page/mock-{slug}.png`; and each `mocks/mock-{slug}.html` with no row in `mocks/index.html` or no `<img>` on the page pointing at its screenshot.
     - `## Result` or `## Decision` still holding its `TODO:` prompt once today's date is past the end date in `## Window`, the end date read as the last `YYYY-MM-DD` in that section, else the last date it can read; a window whose end date cannot be read is its own finding.
     - A `TODO:` anywhere in the page, `README.md` or a mock.

     With `result`, the findings are reported and step 6 follows, the interview covering only the result.
  4. *Interview* by invoking `jodysalt:grill-me` with the subject "the landing page at `docs/landing-page/index.html` and its market test at `docs/landing-page/README.md`". Framing: seed the decision tree with the findings from step 3, then the decisions the page makes without saying so (a price with no terms, a FAQ that skips an objection the terms imply, a headline that promises what no benefit backs); the files explored in step 2 count as explorable, so a question they answer is never asked and each recommended answer cites what it came from; only an answer the user agrees to lands, a run where every question is declined leaving every file byte for byte unchanged; edit `index.html` and `README.md` as answers land, keeping the page's shape – its section ids and order, inline CSS only, no `<link>` and no `<script>` but a user-supplied analytics snippet, every `<img src>` a bare `mock-{slug}.png` beside the page, `og:title`, `og:description` and `og:image` following the headline, the subhead and the hero – and a deferred answer as a `TODO:` marker in its section. When an answer needs a screen the page does not have, or changes what a screen shows, ask for that screen as `add-landing-page` does – its short name whose kebab-case form is the `{slug}`, what it shows and in what state, the benefit it illustrates, desktop (`1280,800`) or phone (`390,844`), desktop recommended – and run step 5 for that one screen as the answer lands, adding or correcting the page's `<img src="mock-{slug}.png" alt="…">`; when the finding is a screenshot missing for a mock that exists, ask only its size and run step 5 for that screen, which leaves the mock untouched and takes the picture. When the user says the test is over, run step 6. The glossary is read, never written.
  5. *Write the mocks.* One sentence of this skill's own first: when the answer rewrites a screen whose `mocks/mock-{slug}.html` exists, that one file is removed first so the step below writes it afresh, and its `<li>` in `mocks/index.html` is rewritten when its name or description changed; every other mock, `mock.css` and `mocks/index.html` keep the step's never-overwrite rule. Then the text of `add-landing-page`'s step 5, from `For each screen, in order, with its slug, name, description, state and size` through the end of its fourth bullet, copied byte for byte: the mock, the shared files on first use, the index row, the browser lookup and the screenshot or the printed command. A re-taken screenshot overwrites the `.png`, which is what the step's command does.
  6. *Record the result.* With `result`, or when the user says the test is over, ask through the same interview for the metric's value at the window's end (recommended: none; the number is the user's), then write `## Result` as the value against the threshold in one line – the metric, its value, the threshold and whether it was met – and ask the decision, the three options being the prompt's own: `build`, `change the offer`, `stop`; recommended `build` when the threshold was met, `stop` when it was not, `change the offer` when the interview finds the page misrepresented the idea rather than the idea failing. Write `## Decision` as the one word or phrase and a sentence of why. A `## Result` already filled is shown as it stands and rewritten only when the answer says so.
  7. *Report.* The findings resolved and those left open; the files edited (`docs/landing-page/index.html`, `docs/landing-page/README.md`); each mock written or rewritten as `mocks/mock-{slug}.html`, `mocks/mock.css` and `mocks/index.html` when this run created them, each screenshot taken at `docs/landing-page/mock-{slug}.png`, and, for each not taken, the one command to run, saying the page already points at that file and, once, that a macOS user substitutes `/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome` for `google-chrome`; when the result was recorded, that `## Result` and `## Decision` now hold it and `jodysalt:refine-vision` cites them. Everything is uncommitted; suggest `jodysalt:commit` – a `feat:` commit when the page or a mock changed, a `docs:` commit when only the notes did.
- **Rules**: never creates `docs/landing-page/index.html`; a missing page stops the run before anything is written. Writes nothing outside `docs/landing-page/`. Never edits `.github/workflows/landing-page.yml`. Rewrites only the mock the answer names; `mock.css`, `mocks/index.html` beyond that mock's row, and every other mock are never overwritten. The screenshot command is the only program run beyond `git` and `date`, and nothing is installed. Never invents copy, a result or a decision; asks, or leaves a `TODO:` marker. Never pushes, never commits, never enables GitHub Pages, never picks a payment, form or analytics provider. Reads `docs/glossary.md` and never writes it.
- **README row** for `refine-landing-page` between `refine-coding-standards` and `refine-sandbox`, its text the description's first sentence.

# Implementation notes
- Draft through `jodysalt:add-skill`'s template by hand rather than running the skill, since this slice holds every answer; keep its paragraph-argument-steps-rules shape and the description in three parts. The argument section is `## Scope`, as `refine-vision` and `refine-sandbox` name theirs.
- Step 4's framing copies `refine-vision` step 4's shape – seeded with findings, the explored files as explorable, only agreed answers land, edit as answers land – plus the page-shape constraints lifted from `add-landing-page` step 3, so an edit never breaks what the parent fixed; put the screen question's wording and the two sizes in the step text, as `add-landing-page` does, so the skill author need not remember them.
- **The mock step is the one piece copied verbatim**: open `skills/add-landing-page/SKILL.md`, take step 5 from `For each screen, in order,` to the end of its fourth bullet, and paste it unchanged after this skill's one lead-in sentence about the mock being rewritten. The lead-in is what lets the copied never-overwrite sentence stand while refine, the editing skill, still rewrites the one mock the answer names: the file is removed before the copied step runs, the row already exists so it is not re-appended (and is rewritten by the lead-in only when its text changed), and `--screenshot` overwrites the `.png`. A missing screenshot for an existing mock is the same step run for that screen: the mock is left untouched, the row stands, the picture is taken. Write the extraction as a `diff <(sed -n …) <(sed -n …)` in the test plan so the copy is checked, not eyeballed; if the paste shows a defect in step 5, report it and leave `add-landing-page` alone.
- The findings mirror `add-landing-page`'s shape rather than the notes' headings one for one: the seven ids are the sections, the five `##` headings are the notes, and only `## Call to action` (against `#cta`'s `href`) and `## Hypothesis` (against the benefits' `<h3>`s) have a page counterpart to check; `## Threshold` and `## Window` have none and are checked only for being filled. The window's end date is read as an ISO date because `add-landing-page` writes dates without fixing a format; `date +%F` is the only date read from the machine.
- The decision's three options are the words `add-landing-page`'s `## Decision` prompt already holds – `build, change the offer, or stop` – so the prompt and its answer match.
- The `result` branch and the whole-page run share one interview: with `result` the findings are reported for the record but only the result is asked; in a whole-page run the result is asked when the window's finding fires and the user confirms the test is over, or when they say so.
- The README row's text is the description's first sentence, inserted after line 41 so the table stays alphabetical.
