# Goal
A user plans against goals and principles instead of bets. `docs/vision.md` names the outcomes it commits to under `## Goals`, one `###` per goal, and the rules every change is judged by under `## Product principles`. A `feat` ticket says what it delivers with `delivers:`, the exact title of a goal, or stands on a principle or core problem named in its `## Strategic fit`. The word "bet" leaves the skills, the README, the scaffold and this repo's vision, where *Dogfooding* becomes a principle and the two remaining bets become goals.

# Context
- Today a `feat` cites a bet with `bet:`, the exact `###` title under `## Strategic bets`, and a `spike` may. Six skills lean on that match. `add-ticket` step 3 is a bet gate that stops when no bet fits until the user picks stand alone, drop, or a new bet for `refine-vision`; step 4 reads spike findings by matching `bet:`; step 6 writes `bet:` in both templates. `refine-ticket` step 2 reads `## Strategic bets`, step 4 flags a `bet:` no heading matches and one on a sub-ticket, and step 6 and its rules make a retag a user decision. `refine-vision` step 2 names `strategic bets` as an argument, step 3 quotes the template prompts and flags a dangling `bet:`, step 4 keeps the five headings and one `###` per bet and rewrites `bet:` tags when a bet is renamed, and its rules say the same. `setup-skills` step 2 writes `## Strategic bets` into the vision template and step 4 writes "names its strategic bets" and "slices a bet into tickets" into the `CLAUDE.md` block. `deliver-brief` step 3's scout reports the bet that covers a `feat`, step 4 runs a `refine-vision` stage that adds a bet for a `feat` with none, and its `add-ticket` stage passes the bet so the gate takes no round.
- The evidence for the change is this repo's own vision. Of its three bets, *Dogfooding* has no end state: "no change to a skill lands without a ticket behind it" is a standing rule of the same kind as *`main` is always installable*, which is already a principle. *Evals for the risky skills* and *Adoption beyond the first user* each name what is true when they are done. Every skill-change ticket so far has tagged *Dogfooding*, including `sub-tickets`, so the gate has been satisfied by a catch-all and has said nothing.
- A ticket cannot deliver a principle. A principle constrains how a change is built; a goal is an outcome a change moves. `## Strategic fit` already has to trace to a principle or a named core problem, so a `feat` with no goal has a trace without a tag.
- No spike in this repo carries `bet:`, and both repos have no open ticket but this one. One closed ticket here, `sub-tickets`, carries `bet:`; the older ones carry `initiative:` and are left alone. Renaming the key keeps its value, and nothing reads a closed ticket's frontmatter.
- `plugin.json` is `0.8.0` and bumps on any change to a skill's behaviour. `README.md` rows 28 and 42 and its Workflow paragraph, this repo's `CLAUDE.md` and the thesis and *Decisions evaporate* problem in `docs/vision.md` say "bet". `claude plugin validate . ` and `claude plugin validate skills --strict` gate the manifest and frontmatter.

# Scope

In:
- **The `delivers:` tag.** A `feat` or a `spike` may carry `delivers:`, the exact title of a `###` heading in `docs/vision.md`, wherever that heading sits. A sub-ticket never carries it and inherits its parent's trace. `bet:` is written nowhere and read nowhere, and every `bet:` line under `docs/tickets/`, this ticket and the closed ones included, becomes `delivers:` with its value unchanged.
- **The goal gate.** `add-ticket` on a `feat` reads the `###` headings and picks the goal the feature delivers. When none fits but a product principle or core problem does, it drafts with no `delivers:` and `## Strategic fit` names what it serves, with no question. When neither fits it stops before writing, and the user picks: drop the feature after the pushback that it may not belong, or name a goal or principle the vision should gain, surfaced for `jodysalt:refine-vision` while the ticket waits. A `spike` tags the goal that fits, or none, and continues. Spike findings are read for a `feat` by matching `delivers:`.
- **Vision shape.** The scaffold writes five `##` headings in order, *Target users*, *Core problems*, *Product principles*, *Goals*, *Non-goals*, with one `###` per goal; those are the defaults. `refine-vision` requires the four bulleted sections by name and, in place of *Goals*, any one `##` section holding `###` headings, whatever it is called, so a repo that keeps `## Strategic bets` or writes `## Outcomes` is not asked to add `## Goals`. Principles stay bulleted. A goal is retired by `refine-vision` when the user says it is met, by deleting its section.
- **Scaffold.** `setup-skills`' vision template writes `## Goals` with the prompt "The outcomes the vision commits to, one `###` section per goal; a `feat` ticket names the goal it delivers by its exact title with `delivers:`." and, under `### {Goal title}`, "What is true when this goal is met, and why it matters now." Its `CLAUDE.md` block opens "`docs/vision.md` sets direction with its goals and principles, `docs/tickets/` slices a goal into tickets and any ticket into `{slug}--{sub}` sub-tickets, …".
- **Templates.** `add-ticket`'s two templates write `delivers: {Exact Goal Title}` where they wrote `bet:`, and the `## Strategic fit` prompt reads "What it delivers toward its goal, or the principle or core problem it stands on. What non-goal it respects."
- **`refine-vision`.** Takes `goals` as a section argument, quotes the new prompts, reports a missing `###` section only when no `##` section holds one, flags a `delivers:` on an open ticket that matches no `###` heading, and rewrites `delivers:` in open tickets when a goal is renamed.
- **`refine-ticket`.** Flags a `delivers:` matching no `###` heading, one on a sub-ticket, and a `feat` with no `delivers:` whose `## Strategic fit` names no principle or core problem. A retag stays a user decision.
- **`deliver-brief`.** The scout reports, for a `feat`, the goal the brief delivers by exact title or, when none fits, the principle or core problem it serves, or that nothing in the vision does. The root ratifies and never adds a goal: the `refine-vision` stage runs only when the scout reported template prompts, filling the vision from the brief, a goal for the brief among its `## Goals` when the ticket is a `feat`. The `add-ticket` stage's argument carries the goal, or the principle the feature stands on, so it takes the gate without a round. A brief that fits nothing stops the run with a report, as the gate stops.
- **This repo's vision.** `## Strategic bets` becomes `## Goals`. *Dogfooding* moves to `## Product principles` as "**Dogfooding.** This repo runs its own workflow: its purpose lives in this file, changes to the skills arrive as tickets, and the loop implements them. No change to a skill lands without a ticket behind it." *Evals for the risky skills* and *Adoption beyond the first user* keep their text. The thesis and *Decisions evaporate* say goals in place of bets.
- **Wording.** `README.md`'s Workflow paragraph and this repo's `CLAUDE.md` take the scaffold's sentence; the rows of every skill whose description changes follow. The README's `## Upgrading from 0.7` section goes and no `## Upgrading from 0.8` replaces it: the maintainer is the only user and migrates by hand. No skill, the README or either `CLAUDE.md` block says "bet" afterwards.
- `plugin.json` to `0.9.0`.

Out:
- Rewriting closed tickets beyond that one key. Their bodies still say "bet" and stay as project memory.
- Migrating the first user's repo, or writing a README note on how; it is done by hand.
- Renaming the four bulleted sections; only the `###` section's name is free. A field for principles, a status on goals, a `delivers:` with more than one value, or a check that `## Strategic fit` names a principle by exact bold title; that stays the judgment it is today.
- Eval suites.
- Pushing.

## Strategic fit
Advances *Dogfooding*, its last ticket as a bet: the change that turns it into a principle arrives as a ticket the loop implements. It serves *Decisions evaporate* from `vision.md`, since a ticket now says what it delivers in a word the vision also uses, and *Conventions are hard to adopt*, since a newcomer reasons about goals and principles rather than a word that needs explaining. It follows *One opinionated method* and *Trim with the 80/20 principle* by renaming a field and generalising a match rather than adding a layer, and it moves *Adoption beyond the first user* closer for the same reason. It respects the *Several plugins* non-goal, since nothing leaves the one plugin.
